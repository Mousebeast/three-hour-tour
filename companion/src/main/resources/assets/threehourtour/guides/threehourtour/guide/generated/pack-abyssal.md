# Abyssal packs

You first need these at **Ruin Archaeology** — a tier 3 node on the depth track, which costs 15 of them. 29 nodes want this pack in all.

First you need <ItemLink id="create:copper_diving_helmet" />, <ItemLink id="create:copper_backtank" />.

## How one is made

Three steps. The first two are the same for every pack; the third is this pack's own, and no two packs are finished the same way.

- **4 gathered items** → **Crafting** at <ItemLink id="minecraft:crafting_table" /> → <ItemLink id="threehourtour:bundle_abyssal" />
- **3 ×** <ItemLink id="threehourtour:bundle_abyssal" /> → **Mixing** at <ItemLink id="create:basin" /> and <ItemLink id="create:mechanical_mixer" /> → <ItemLink id="threehourtour:core_abyssal" />
- <ItemLink id="threehourtour:core_abyssal" /> → **Washing** at <ItemLink id="create:encased_fan" /> → <ItemLink id="threehourtour:pack_abyssal" />

## Other ways to fill a bundle

A shortcut shortens the gathering. The last step is unchanged.

### Brushed Artefacts

Assembled by **Crafting** at <ItemLink id="minecraft:crafting_table" />.

| Ingredient | Per pack | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| anything counted as Decorated Pot Sherds | 3 | Diving |
| <ItemLink id="minecraft:prismarine_shard" /> | 3 | Diving |
| <ItemLink id="minecraft:sponge" /> | 3 | Diving |

**9 items instead of 12.** The gathering is shorter; the last step is the same.
