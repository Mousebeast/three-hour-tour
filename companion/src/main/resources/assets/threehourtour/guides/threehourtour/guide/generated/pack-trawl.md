# Trawl packs

You first need these at **Flotsam Handling** — a tier 1 node on the hull track, which costs 9 of them. 74 nodes want this pack in all.

Everything this pack asks for can be gathered on day one, with no machine and nothing unlocked.

## How one is made

Three steps. The first two are the same for every pack; the third is this pack's own, and no two packs are finished the same way.

- **9 gathered items** → **Crafting** at <ItemLink id="minecraft:crafting_table" /> → <ItemLink id="threehourtour:bundle_trawl" />
- **3 ×** <ItemLink id="threehourtour:bundle_trawl" /> → **Crafting** at <ItemLink id="minecraft:crafting_table" /> → <ItemLink id="threehourtour:core_trawl" />
- <ItemLink id="threehourtour:core_trawl" /> → **Crafting** at <ItemLink id="minecraft:crafting_table" /> → <ItemLink id="threehourtour:pack_trawl" />

That last step needs no machine at all. You can finish this one on a bare raft.

## Other ways to fill a bundle

A shortcut shortens the gathering. The last step is unchanged.

### Trading Hall

Assembled by **Crafting** at <ItemLink id="minecraft:crafting_table" />.

| Ingredient | Per pack | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| <ItemLink id="minecraft:emerald_block" /> | 3 | Trading |
| <ItemLink id="minecraft:dried_kelp" /> | 3 | Sea scoop |
| <ItemLink id="minecraft:seagrass" /> | 3 | Sea scoop |
| <ItemLink id="minecraft:cooked_cod" /> | 3 | Fishing |
| <ItemLink id="minecraft:string" /> | 3 | Sea scoop |

**15 items instead of 27.** The gathering is shorter; the last step is the same.
