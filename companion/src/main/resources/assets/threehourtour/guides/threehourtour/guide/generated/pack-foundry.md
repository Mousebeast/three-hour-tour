# Foundry packs

You first need these at **Apothic Enchanting** — a tier 2 node on the combat track, which costs 30 of them. 58 nodes want this pack in all.

Everything this pack asks for can be gathered on day one, with no machine and nothing unlocked.

## How one is made

Three steps. The first two are the same for every pack; the third is this pack's own, and no two packs are finished the same way.

- **9 gathered items** → **Crafting** at <ItemLink id="minecraft:crafting_table" /> → <ItemLink id="threehourtour:bundle_foundry" />
- **3 ×** <ItemLink id="threehourtour:bundle_foundry" /> → **Mixing** at <ItemLink id="create:basin" /> and <ItemLink id="create:mechanical_mixer" /> → <ItemLink id="threehourtour:core_foundry" />
- <ItemLink id="threehourtour:core_foundry" /> → **Sequenced assembly** at <ItemLink id="create:depot" /> and <ItemLink id="create:mechanical_press" /> → <ItemLink id="threehourtour:pack_foundry" />

## Other ways to fill a bundle

A shortcut shortens the gathering. The last step is unchanged.

### Mechanical Crafting

Assembled by **Mechanical crafting** at <ItemLink id="create:mechanical_crafter" />.

| Ingredient | Per pack | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| <ItemLink id="create:andesite_alloy" /> | 6 | Factory |
| <ItemLink id="minecraft:copper_ingot" /> | 9 | Sea scoop |
| <ItemLink id="minecraft:iron_ingot" /> | 6 | Sea scoop |

**21 items instead of 27.** The gathering is shorter; the last step is the same.
