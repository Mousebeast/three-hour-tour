# Metal and ore veins

**There is no ore in the ground.** Not "less ore" — none.

The handful of ore blocks you will still see are decoration inside structures.
They are not a supply, and mining them is a hobby.

Metal comes from **veins** instead: a deposit the size of a chunk column, in a
fixed place, that you find on purpose and can only extract with a
<ItemLink id="createoreexcavation:drilling_machine" />.

## How to actually get metal

Four steps. The first two are the search; the last two are the extraction.

### 1. Hunt with the Ore Vein Finder

<ItemLink id="createoreexcavation:vein_finder" /> is a handheld you use while
moving. It tells you one of three things: nothing, *traces of* something — which
means a vein of that kind is somewhere in range but not here — or *found
nearby*, which means it is in this chunk or the one beside it. There is a five
second cooldown between uses, so sail slowly and use it often.

**It needs no fuel and never wears out.** Craft it once and it is yours for the
rest of the game.

**Carry an <ItemLink id="createoreexcavation:vein_atlas" /> and it narrows.**
Target a vein type in the atlas and the finder reports only that one, ignoring
everything else — which is the difference between hunting for mithril and being
told about coal for an hour. Its tooltip says *filtered using the Atlas* when
this is on.

### 2. Confirm with a Sample Drill

<ItemLink id="createoreexcavation:sample_drill" /> is placed on the ground and
tells you exactly what is under it. It needs a
<ItemLink id="create:copper_backtank" /> sitting **on top of it** as an air
supply — it will refuse to run without one and will tell you so.

Let it finish a full cycle, then click it with an
<ItemLink id="createoreexcavation:vein_atlas" /> to record the vein. The atlas
is your map of everywhere you have been; without it you are relying on memory
and coordinates.

### 3. Drill it

<ItemLink id="createoreexcavation:drilling_machine" /> is the machine that
empties a vein. It needs three things:

- **Rotational force**, like any Create machine.
- **A drill inside it** — <ItemLink id="createoreexcavation:drill" />,
  <ItemLink id="createoreexcavation:diamond_drill" /> or
  <ItemLink id="createoreexcavation:netherite_drill" />. A better drill is
  faster, and some veins refuse a drill that is too weak.
- **Drilling fluid**, for the veins that ask for it. The machine will say so.

If it complains that there is no vein, you are not over one — the machine works
on the chunk it is standing in, not on the block under it.

### 4. Take the output

Pull the results out of the machine like any Create block. You can leave it
running: these veins do not run dry.

## Build a drilling platform

Most veins you find will be under open water, and a drilling machine needs
somewhere to stand. Build it one: a small platform over the vein carrying the
machine, its rotation, and a container for the output.

Then sail off and come back to a full chest. It is the only structure in the
pack worth leaving behind you.

## Nether metal, in the overworld

Several veins that would normally be Nether-only generate here instead —
quartz, glowstone, nether gold, ancient debris. The Nether is closed, so the
ore that lived there had to move.

Everything from the Nether that is *not* ore — blaze rods, wither skulls, ghast
tears, bartering — comes through the gates. See
[The Nether and the End](closed-doors.md).
