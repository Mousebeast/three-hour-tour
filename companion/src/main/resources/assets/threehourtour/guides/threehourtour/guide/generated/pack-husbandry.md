# Husbandry packs

You first need these at **Heavy Ordnance** — a tier 4 node on the combat track, which costs 60 of them. 16 nodes want this pack in all.

First you need <ItemLink id="animalhusbandry:feeding_trough" />.

## How one is made

Three steps. The first two are the same for every pack; the third is this pack's own, and no two packs are finished the same way.

- **9 gathered items** → **Crafting** at <ItemLink id="minecraft:crafting_table" /> → <ItemLink id="threehourtour:bundle_husbandry" />
- **8 ×** <ItemLink id="threehourtour:bundle_husbandry" /> → **Compacting** at <ItemLink id="create:basin" /> and <ItemLink id="create:mechanical_press" /> → <ItemLink id="threehourtour:core_husbandry" />
- <ItemLink id="threehourtour:core_husbandry" /> → **Cutting** at <ItemLink id="farmersdelight:cutting_board" /> → <ItemLink id="threehourtour:pack_husbandry" />

**Cutting** is not available until you have researched *Ship’s Galley*.
