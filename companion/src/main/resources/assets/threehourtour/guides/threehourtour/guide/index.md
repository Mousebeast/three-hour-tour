# You are a castaway

You set out for three hours. You are not going home.

What you have is a raft, the sea under it, and whatever you can pull out of the
water.

This book explains the parts of that which are **not** how Minecraft normally
works. It does not explain crafting tables. It explains why your scoop produces
nothing while you sit at anchor, why mining a mountain gives you no ore, why the
water you are floating on will make you ill, and why standing still with a full
belly does not heal you.

## How to read it

**Hold `G` over any item** to open the page that explains it. That works for
anything this pack adds — a research pack, a gate pearl, a trophy — and it is
faster than finding the page by hand.

The pages on the left are grouped by where you will meet them. Each one opens
to show what is under it.

- [Your ship](ship.md) — you get one, it is your home, it has to float, and it
  is where you heal.
- [The sea](sea.md) — how you earn, why you cannot drink, and what islands are
  for.
- [Research](research.md) — the spine of the pack, and the six kinds of pack
  you pay it with.
- [Gates and bosses](gates.md) — the second economy, the one that runs on
  fights.
- [Metal and ore veins](ore.md) — there is no ore in the ground; here is what
  replaced it.
- [The Nether and the End](closed-doors.md) — both shut, and what stands in for
  each thing you would have gone there for.

**Most of the tables in this book are generated** from the same files the game
itself is built from. If a number appears in one, it is the number the game is
using.

## The short version

- You get **one ship**, for the whole game. The lever on the raft is
  irreversible.
- **The sea is the road.** Your scoop only works while you are moving.
- **Nothing moves until something burns.** The engine arrives empty and takes
  any furnace fuel. The bonsai pot in your chest is the half that never runs
  out, and it is the only thing aboard that works while you sit still.
- **Islands are for finding things**, never for living on. There is no ore in
  the ground; metal comes from veins you locate and drill.
- **Research is the spine.** You gather, you make research packs, you spend them
  at a terminal on your ship, and things unlock.
- **You do not heal by yourself.** Natural regeneration is off; your ship heals
  you, and what you carry ashore is what you have.
- **The Nether and the End are shut.** Everything you would have gone there
  for has another route.
