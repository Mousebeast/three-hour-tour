# Gates and bosses

There is a whole second economy in this pack, and it runs on fights.

The short version: you craft a **gate pearl**, drop it on open ground, and an
arena forms around it. Waves of a particular faction come at you. Survive them
all and the gate pays out — always <ItemLink id="threehourtour:ash" />, and
usually a material besides. Then the pearl is spent and you craft another.

## Why you would bother

Three reasons, and they are all things you cannot get any other way.

- **Ash.** It is the fuel of this entire side of the game, and nothing produces
  it but a cleared gate. No machine mints it.
- **Materials from the Nether and the End.** Both are shut. Nether wart, end
  stone, ancient debris and the rest come out of the factions that would have
  lived there.
- **Arcane packs.** Every Arcane bundle contains ash, on every recipe path. It
  is the one research pack you cannot buy with a gathering trip.

Ash is also what boss gates cost, so **research and boss-hunting compete for the
same pile of it.**

## The three grades

Every faction's pearl comes in three grades, and they are the same shape
everywhere.

| Grade | Arena | Waves | Ash it pays |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | small | 3 | 4 |
| II | medium | 5 | 10 |
| III | large | 7 | 24 |

A pearl recipe has two halves.

- **The outside says which grade.** A grade I pearl is sea goods and nothing
  else, so you can make one on day one without a single machine. Grade II adds
  an iron sheet. Grade III adds ash — which means the third grade costs you a
  clear of the second.
- **The middle slot says which faction.** One item, the same at all three
  grades, different for every faction. Change that one slot and the same grid
  opens a different arena.

That middle slot is why some factions are locked behind others. Most of them are
keyed on something you can go and find. Three are keyed on a **sigil**, and a
sigil drops from one specific other faction's gates — so there is no route to
those three except through the faction that pays for them.

## What a boss costs

Ten of the twelve bosses live behind a **Named Gate**: a pearl bought with ash
and an arena pearl of that boss's own faction. The other two swim around in the
open ocean, free of charge, waiting.

Each boss drops **its own trophy**, and every trophy crafts one-for-one into one
of four **seals**. Research asks for seals, never for a boss by name — see
**Trophy pools**.
