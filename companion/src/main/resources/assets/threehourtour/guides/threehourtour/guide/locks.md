# What a lock actually is

Buying a node does not hand you an item. It grants you a **stage**, and recipes
and items check that stage.

That has three consequences worth knowing before you spend anything.

## Nothing is hidden

Every locked item stays in the recipe browser, visible, with a note saying which
node opens it. There is no secret list and no discovery puzzle. If you want to
know whether something exists, look it up — you will find it, and you will find
out what it costs.

## A lock is always a capability

A node opens a **step up in what you can do**: a machine tier, a fluid tier, a
subsystem you did not have. Never decoration, never a single convenience item
you would otherwise have crafted for a few sticks, and never gear.

If a node looks like it is selling you a trinket, it is a tier upgrade wearing a
trinket's shape — a better drill, a better mesh, a better controller.

## Some things are never locked

Vanilla. Create's basic tier — the basin, mixer, press, fan, millstone, crushing
wheels and depot. Your sea scoop. The research terminal. The tree-growing pots.

You have all of it from the first minute. Four of the six research packs are
made on those machines, so a lock on any of them would mean spending packs to
unlock the tools that make packs.
