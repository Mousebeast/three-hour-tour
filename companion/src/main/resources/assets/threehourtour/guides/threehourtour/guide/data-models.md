# Farming a faction

Clearing gates by hand is how you start. It is not how you want to be spending
your evenings at the far end of the tree, and the pack has an answer: teach a
machine to have the fight for you.

Seven of the factions have a **data model**. Each faction's own page shows its
numbers; this page is how the system works.

## The loop

- **Kill the faction's creatures** while holding a blank model, and it learns.
- **Put the trained model in a simulation chamber** and feed it power. Each
  simulation always returns an Overworld Prediction.
- **Roll for the good one.** On top of that guaranteed output, there is a chance
  of a Mob Prediction, and the chance is how well the model has learned.
- **Run a prediction through a loot fabricator** and it pays out that faction's
  materials.

## Learning is steep, then flat

| The model is | After this many kills | Chance of a prediction |
|---|---|---|
| Faulty | 0 | 0% |
| Basic | 6 | 5% |
| Advanced | 54 | 22% |
| Superior | 354 | 65% |
| Self-aware | 1254 | 100% |

Six kills gets you a model that technically works. Fifty-four gets you one worth
running. Superior is where it becomes genuinely productive. Self-aware is a
lifestyle choice.

**A model is trained by the faction's own creatures**, not by anything that
happens to be in its arena. The vanilla mobs padding out a wave roster teach it
nothing.

## The Overworld has no model

Every mob on the Overworld roster already has a data model shipped by Hostile
Neural Networks itself. Use that one.

## What this does not replace

A data model pays **materials**. It does not pay ash, and it does not pay
trophies. Gates and bosses still have to be fought by somebody. What the model
buys you is never having to grind a faction for its nether wart again.
