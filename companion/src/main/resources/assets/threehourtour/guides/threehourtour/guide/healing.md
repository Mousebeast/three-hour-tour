# Staying alive

**You do not heal by standing around with a full belly.** Natural regeneration
is switched off. Eating keeps you fed and lets you sprint, and it does nothing
whatsoever for your health bar.

What heals you is your ship.

## The deck mends you

**Standing on a vessel heals you, slowly.** Any vessel — yours, a teammate's,
anything with a core under it. There is no condition attached: you do not need
to be fed, you do not need to be still, and nothing switches it off.

It is deliberately slow. Roughly half the rate of a Regeneration potion, which
is a couple of minutes from nearly dead back to full at the start of the game
and rather longer once you have earned some hearts. It is not something you
stand and wait for. It is the reason the deck is where you want to be.

This is what makes the ship your home in a way the story alone could not. Going
ashore now costs something, and coming back is the relief.

## What you carry ashore

Away from the deck you have exactly what you brought.

**Comfort** is the one to know about. It is a Farmer's Delight effect —
"provides natural regeneration, regardless of how hungry you are" — and it
still works with vanilla regeneration off. It comes from drinks, which means it
comes from your galley. A cooked drink in your pack is the difference between a
bad landfall and a walk home.

**Potions and golden apples** remain what they always were: the emergency, for
when Comfort is not enough or has run out.

So healing has three tiers and you will use all three:

| | What it costs | Where |
|---|---|---|
| The deck | Nothing | Aboard any vessel |
| Comfort | A cooked drink | Anywhere, while it lasts |
| Potions | Brewing, or an apple you were saving | Anywhere, immediately |

## Food has two other jobs instead

It stopped being your healing. It gained two better roles.

**Eating widely raises your maximum health.** Your hearts are earned by
*variety*, not quantity — the count of genuinely different foods you have eaten
over the whole save. You start at ten hearts and reach twenty, two at a time, at
five, ten, fifteen, twenty and twenty-five distinct foods. Eating your
forty-first loaf of bread does nothing. The one new thing you have never tried
does.

That list is not reset when you die, so nothing you have learned to eat is ever
lost.

**And a proper meal buys you time on thirst.** The Nourishment effect from a
cooked dish pauses your thirst draining — see *Drinking water*. It does not
refill it; nothing does. It stops the clock for a while.

Between the two, the galley is not a decoration. It is where your health
ceiling, your portable healing and your thirst timer all come from.

## The short version

- You do not regenerate. The ship does it for you.
- Take a Comfort drink ashore, every time.
- Eat something you have never eaten before whenever you get the chance.
