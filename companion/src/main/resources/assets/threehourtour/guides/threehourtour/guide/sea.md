# The sea

The ocean is not the space between the interesting places. It is the road and
the larder.

**You earn by moving.** The sea scoop harvests while your ship is under way and
produces nothing at anchor. Sailing is not travel time; it is the work.

**You cannot drink it.** You are surrounded by water and none of it is safe.
There is a real answer to that, and it involves a kettle and some patience.

**Land is a shop, not a home.** You go ashore for things you cannot get at sea,
and then you leave.

## What is out there

Ocean monuments are larger and randomised. Ocean dungeons and shipwrecks carry
the early loot. Ocean ruins hold suspicious sand for archaeology — and those
blocks destroy what is inside them if you break them, so the material only comes
out by brushing in place. You cannot haul a dig site home.

Buried treasure is reachable without squinting at a map. The difficulty is
having the gear to get that deep, not decoding the chart.

Land is sparse and the deep is genuinely deep: continents at a quarter scale,
oceans nearly two and a half times deeper than vanilla, and land biased away
from the middle of the map. The centre of the world is open water, all the way
down.
