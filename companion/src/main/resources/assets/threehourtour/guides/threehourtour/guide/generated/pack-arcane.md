# Arcane packs

You first need these at **Jewelcrafting** — a tier 3 node on the magic track, which costs 29 of them. 7 nodes want this pack in all.

Everything this pack asks for can be gathered on day one, with no machine and nothing unlocked.

## How one is made

Three steps. The first two are the same for every pack; the third is this pack's own, and no two packs are finished the same way.

- **9 gathered items** → **Crafting** at <ItemLink id="minecraft:crafting_table" /> → <ItemLink id="threehourtour:bundle_arcane" />
- **7 ×** <ItemLink id="threehourtour:bundle_arcane" /> → **Mixing** at <ItemLink id="create:basin" /> and <ItemLink id="create:mechanical_mixer" /> → <ItemLink id="threehourtour:core_arcane" />
- <ItemLink id="threehourtour:core_arcane" /> → **Infusion** at <ItemLink id="minecraft:enchanting_table" /> → <ItemLink id="threehourtour:pack_arcane" />

**Infusion** is not available until you have researched *Apothic Enchanting*.

## Other ways to fill a bundle

A shortcut shortens the gathering. The last step is unchanged.
