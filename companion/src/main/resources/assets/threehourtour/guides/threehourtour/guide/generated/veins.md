# Vein types

Every kind of deposit in this pack, sorted by how hard it is to find. The distance is how far apart two deposits of that kind sit on average — so it is roughly how far you sail between one and the next.

Every vein here is renewable — the atlas will say **Renewable (∞)** when you record one. None of them can be emptied.


## Everywhere

Hard to miss.

| Vein | What it gives | One roughly every |
|---|---|---|
| Water | <ItemLink id="minecraft:water_bucket" /> | 64 blocks |
