# Islands and landfall

**The ship is where things get made. Islands are where things get found.**

Nothing forbids you visiting, and nothing rewards you staying. Islands are
sized for the job: big enough to hold something worth a voyage, small enough
that nobody is tempted to build a summer home.

## What you can only get ashore

- **Seeds and saplings.** Then you grow them on deck.
- **Breeding stock.** Then you breed them on deck.
- **Wool**, which is the gate on bulk clean water.
- **Stone**, and everything downstream of it — a furnace, bricks, and the whole
  Create line. There is none at sea.
- **Sand**, which is not in the water either, and which one faction is keyed on.

Two of the six research packs need something from land. The other four stay
reachable from the water.

## What you will not find ashore

**Ore.** Mining an island gives you stone and disappointment — see
*Metal and ore veins*.

## Everyone shares one ocean

There are no per-player dimensions and no lobby. Everyone is in the same
overworld, spawned on a grid a few thousand blocks apart.

One ocean is what lets ships sail to each other, teammates meet at sea, and
islands be worth contesting. It is also what lets somebody moor next to you at
three in the morning.
