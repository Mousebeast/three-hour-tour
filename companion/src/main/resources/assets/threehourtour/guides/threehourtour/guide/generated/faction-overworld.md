# Overworld

## Opening a gate

Craft a gate pearl, drop it on open ground, and an arena forms. Survive the waves and the gate pays out.

- **I** — a small arena, 3 waves, pays **4 ×** <ItemLink id="threehourtour:ash" />. Costs 4 × <ItemLink id="minecraft:kelp" />, 2 × <ItemLink id="minecraft:prismarine_shard" />, 3 × <ItemLink id="minecraft:string" />.
- **II** — a medium arena, 5 waves, pays **10 ×** <ItemLink id="threehourtour:ash" />. Costs 2 × <ItemLink id="create:iron_sheet" />, 4 × <ItemLink id="minecraft:kelp" />, 2 × <ItemLink id="minecraft:prismarine_shard" />, 1 × <ItemLink id="minecraft:string" />.
- **III** — a large arena, 7 waves, pays **24 ×** <ItemLink id="threehourtour:ash" />. Costs 2 × <ItemLink id="create:iron_sheet" />, 2 × <ItemLink id="minecraft:kelp" />, 2 × <ItemLink id="minecraft:prismarine_shard" />, 1 × <ItemLink id="minecraft:string" />, 2 × <ItemLink id="threehourtour:ash" />.

These recipes need no key item — this is the one faction anyone can open from the first day, out of nothing but what the sea gives you.

## Who turns up

- Zombie
- Skeleton
- Creeper
- Spider
- Drowned
- Witch

## What clearing it is worth

Beyond the ash, the gate pays <ItemLink id="minecraft:gunpowder" />.

## Its boss

- **Wither**, behind its own Named Gate pearl. Drops <ItemLink id="threehourtour:trophy_wither" />, which crafts into <ItemLink id="threehourtour:seal_alpha" />.
