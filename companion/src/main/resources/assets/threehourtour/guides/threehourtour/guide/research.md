# Research

Research is the spine of this pack. Almost everything you cannot do yet is
waiting behind it, and the way through is always the same: go and do something,
turn what you gathered into **research packs**, and spend them.

You spend them at a <ItemLink id="threehourtour:research_terminal" />, which has
to be aboard your ship. There is no research on land.

**The terminal shows its work on its own face.** Whatever node you have pinned
is written on the front of the block — its name, and a line per thing it wants
with how many you have against how many it needs, yellow while you are short
and green once you are not. You do not have to open anything; walk past and
look. It reads the same data the tree does, so the two cannot disagree.

## The tree

Press <KeyBind id="key.ftbquests.quests" /> to open it. One canvas,
seventy-four nodes, eight tracks, five tiers. Buying a node does not give you an
item — it gives you **access**. A machine tier, a fluid tier, sometimes a whole
mod that was closed until now.

Three things about it are worth knowing before you start.

- **Nothing is hidden.** Every locked item stays in the recipe browser and tells
  you which node opens it. There is no secret list.
- **A lock is always a step up in what you can do** — a better machine, a new
  fluid, a subsystem you did not have. Never decoration, never gear.
- **Some things are never locked at all.** Vanilla, Create's basic tier, your
  sea scoop, this terminal and the tree pots are yours from the first minute.

## Six kinds of pack, and they do not substitute

The six are **not** six tiers of one thing, and there is no exchange rate
between them. They are six different ways of spending an afternoon, and a node
that asks for Abyssal cannot be paid in Trawl no matter how much Trawl you have
or how nicely you ask.

## How any of them is made

Three steps, always in this order.

- Gather nine raw things and craft them into a **bundle**. Repeat.
- Combine several bundles into a **core**.
- Finish the core into a **pack**. This last step is the pack's **signature**,
  and every pack has a different one.

**Trawl needs no machine at any step.** It is the first pack the tree asks for,
and a crafting table and a full scoop is the whole shopping list.

## Shortcuts

Some packs have a second recipe. The rule behind every one of them is a single
sentence:

> You built infrastructure, so this is cheaper.

A shortcut never removes the activity the pack exists to make you do. It makes
the gathering shorter.

## The far end costs kills

The deepest nodes cannot be bought with gathering at all. They want proof that
bosses have died — see [Gates and bosses](gates.md).
