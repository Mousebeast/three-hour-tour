# Your ship

You get **one ship**. Not one at a time — one, for the whole game.

Your first act is pulling the lever wired to the Physics Assembler on the
starting raft. The raft becomes a live vessel, the assembler is consumed and
replaced in place by the **ship core**, and the core binds to you.

**There is no recipe for a second assembler.** After that lever, there is no
building another vessel. This one is your home, your workshop, your farm and
the place progression happens.

## The core is the ship

The vessel's identity lives in the core, and the core is unusually hard to
lose. It cannot be destroyed by normal means, cannot be carried off, cannot be
pushed by machinery, and it rides along automatically if the ship is split in
two. Blow the deck to splinters and the core still floats, bobbing there,
judging you.

## You can move it, though

It never becomes an item, so nobody can take it — but you are not stuck with
wherever it ended up.

- **Sneak and use the core with an empty hand.** It says you are moving it.
- **Use any block on the same ship** and the core sets itself down against
  that face.
- **Sneak-use the core again** to change your mind.

Two rules: it stays on its own ship, and the space you pick has to be empty.
If either fails the core tells you which, and stays exactly where it was.

## Teams sail together

Teammates each keep their own ship. A team is a fleet, not a shared hull. You
can build an outpost together, moor alongside each other and fight the same
gate — you just each go home to your own deck.
