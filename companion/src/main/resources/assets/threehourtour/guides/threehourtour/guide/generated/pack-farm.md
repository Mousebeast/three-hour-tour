# Farm packs

You first need these at **Pylon Fields** — a tier 2 node on the industry track, which costs 13 of them. 22 nodes want this pack in all.

First you need <ItemLink id="botanypots:terracotta_botany_pot" />.

## How one is made

Three steps. The first two are the same for every pack; the third is this pack's own, and no two packs are finished the same way.

- **9 gathered items** → **Crafting** at <ItemLink id="minecraft:crafting_table" /> → <ItemLink id="threehourtour:bundle_farm" />
- **3 ×** <ItemLink id="threehourtour:bundle_farm" /> → **Mixing** at <ItemLink id="create:basin" /> and <ItemLink id="create:mechanical_mixer" /> → <ItemLink id="threehourtour:core_farm" />
- <ItemLink id="threehourtour:core_farm" /> → **Cooking** at <ItemLink id="farmersdelight:cooking_pot" /> → <ItemLink id="threehourtour:pack_farm" />

**Cooking** is not available until you have researched *Ship’s Galley*.
